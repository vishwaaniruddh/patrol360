Patrol360 Intelligence System Analysis

Detailed technical breakdown of the 3-Layer Police Vehicle Architecture, AI capabilities, and Scope of Work for the Commissioner of Police (CP Sir) POC evaluation.

The Three Core Architectural Layers Fully Integrated
1. Vehicle Level (Edge AI)

• 4 IP Cameras: Mounted Front, Rear, Left, and Right on patrol vehicle.
• NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano (16GB): Runs Ubuntu 24.04 and TensorRT engines.
• Industrial Power / UPS: Connected to car battery with isolated DC-DC regulator.

2. Connectivity Level (Rugged 4G/5G)

• Dual-SIM Router: Low-latency real-time event transmission.
• GPS Telemetry: Live tracking of vehicle speed, road location, and heading.
• Offline Store-and-Forward: If signal drops, alerts buffer on local SSD and auto-sync on reconnect.

3. Central Level (Police ICCC Platform)

• Unified Command Console: City-wide map of all active patrol vehicles.
• Tactical Alert Triage: Real-time alerts with photo footprints and 10s video clips.
• Human-In-The-Loop Validation: Verification before dispatching PCR backup vans.

Deep-Dive: The 5 AI Analytics Capabilities Simultaneous Edge Processing
🚗 1. ANPR / ALPR (Number Plates & Stolen Cars)

• Function: Reads high-speed number plates from front & rear cameras.
• Classification: Categorizes Car, Motorcycle, Bus, and Truck.
• Blacklist Matching: Normalized regex matching against police stolen vehicle registry.
• Payload Sent: Plate number, OCR confidence, vehicle class, snapshot, video clip, GPS.

👤 2. Facial Recognition System (FRS)

• Function: Multi-face tracking per frame from sidewalk flank cameras.
• Watchlist Matching: 512-dimension vector embedding cosine match against criminal & missing persons database.
• Anti-Spoofing: Liveness detection to reject printed photos or phone screens.
• Review Process: Operator verification audit before initiating police pursuit.

🔫 3. Weapon Threat Detection

• Function: Real-time identification of visible firearms (handguns, rifles) and bladed weapons in public spaces.
• Field Alert: Immediate loud in-car audio chime and flashing screen overlay.
• Command Dispatch: Top-priority event push to ICCC with bounding box coordinates.

🔥 4. Fire & Smoke Hazard Detection

• Function: Detects rising smoke plumes and flame hazards from all 4 cameras.
• Rapid Response: Automatically alerts the fire department with exact street GPS coordinates.
• Cooldown Logic: Prevents repeated duplicate alerts for the same continuous incident.

👥 5. Crowd & Object Analytics

• Head Counting: Tracks number of individuals moving across junctions and plazas.
• Overcrowding Management: Triggers warning if pedestrian count exceeds threshold (>40 persons).
• Density Heatmaps: Generates color-coded density maps for city traffic & crowd controllers.
