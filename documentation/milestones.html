✅ Official POC Success & Sign-Off Criteria for CP Sir

The POC will be considered 100% successful when the system satisfies all of the following conditions during the live vehicle demonstration and 7-day field run:
1. Simultaneous 4-Camera Ingestion: Processes Front, Rear, Left, and Right 1080p camera feeds without dropping below 20 FPS.
2. Accurate Alert Generation: Successfully detects number plates, faces, weapons, smoke, and crowd densities with confidence scores.
3. Immediate Evidence Storage: Saves a high-resolution snapshot and 10-second video footprint for every detected incident.
4. Sub-Second ICCC Push: Delivers alerts to the police control room API in under 850ms over 4G/5G.
5. Offline Zero Data Loss: Caches all alerts safely during network drops and syncs seamlessly when connection returns.
